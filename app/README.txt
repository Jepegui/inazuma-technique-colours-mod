TECHNIQUE COLOURS - recoloured special moves for Inazuma Fantasy

  1. Unzip everything first (the app does not work from inside a zip),
     NOT inside the game's mods folder: the app is not a mod.
  2. Open "Technique Colours.bat" (Microsoft Edge) or technique-colours.html
     in Edge or Chrome.
  3. Choose the game's mods folder (the game you start with the mod launcher,
     with VRFW and InazumaFantasy in it).
  4. Search the original move, choose its colour, name and price, and click
     "Create move".
  5. With the game closed, click "Generate the mod", then switch on
     "Technique Colours" in the launcher. Your moves are in Inazuma Fantasy's
     Technique Shop.

  - The app only writes inside mods\TechniqueColours. Nothing leaves your computer.
  - Before you delete a move in the app, take it off the players who learned it.
  - Do not use online play or cross-save with mods.

The name banners use the Rum Raisin font (SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL.txt).
